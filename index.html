💬 How to talk to your assistant

Model 1 understands short, direct messages. Try phrasing like these — they all work:

Mark something done
"I read for an hour"
"practiced guitar" · "done reading"
Log extra time
"I read 45 minutes instead of 30"
Missed one
"skip guitar today" · "didn't read"
Ask what's left
"what's left today?"
Add a goal
"add a goal to drink more water"

👉 In Model 2, you won't need to phrase it carefully — you'll just talk, and it'll understand.
